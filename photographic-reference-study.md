## Neutral validation

Both frozen and rebuilt assets are inspected under the same neutral daylight rig: white directional light, cooler sky fill, neutral ground bounce, shared sky and reflection environment, no fog, bloom, shafts or color grading. This development-only view exposes silhouette, material scale and surface-response problems. The five normal weather presets are compared separately with identical camera positions, journey state and rendering resolution.

## Licensing boundaries

The field-guide photographs retain their existing individual credits and licenses. The external reference screenshots remain in the ignored local research directory. Imported rendering assets use verified permissive sources; their authors, original URLs, licenses, resolution and adaptations are listed in the High asset manifest and adjacent credits. Authored mangrove root/planting adaptations are an artistic interpretation, not scanned Bhitarkanika specimens or surveyed geography.
